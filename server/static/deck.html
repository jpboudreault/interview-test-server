Income tax 101

How brackets work, what the effective rate is, and how our services get each year’s rates.

How income tax works

Your income is taxed in slices

Progressive by design: the more you earn, the higher the rate on the top slice, so higher incomes carry more of the cost of public services.

What to build

A Python endpoint: year + income in, tax out

Endpoint inputs

  • income: annual income, e.g. 100000
  • tax_year: e.g. 2022

Output

{
"total_tax": "17739.17",
"effective_rate": "0.1774",
"bands": [ { "rate": "0.15", "tax": "7529.55" }, … ]
}

Your Python endpoint must get the tax rates from the reference service.
